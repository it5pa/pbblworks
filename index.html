B P B L

is a creative studio specializing in visual identities, publications, website and exhibition design. In our work, we examine the matter at hand like turning over a pebble in your hand, exploring the different surfaces of a project through contextual research and material exploration. Our projects are developed through a collaborative, intuitive and iterative practice.

B L P B

works for and with cultural institutions, artists, curators, creative professionals and small businesses in digital, print media in two and three dimensions.
